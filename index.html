About Ashiana Amodh

Ashiana Amodh is Ashiana Housing's 7th senior living project, located in Talegaon, Pune. Spread over approximately 11.93 acres, it is designed for residents aged 55+, with 1-, 2-, and 3-BHK homes that include age-friendly features like grab rails, skid-resistant tiles and an emergency response system.

The heart of the community is a 19,000 sq ft clubhouse that offers wellness amenities (yoga lawn, fitness centre), recreational spaces (indoor games, library), and social areas. Located near the Indrani River with green hills and excellent connectivity to both Mumbai and Pune, Amodh seeks to foster an "active, happy, and fulfilling" retirement lifestyle.
